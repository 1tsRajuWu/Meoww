# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your data. By inviting the Bot to your server or interacting with it, you agree to the terms outlined below.

## 1. Data We Collect
To provide moderation, ticketing, and server management features, the Bot collects and stores the following information:
*   **Discord IDs:** We store Guild (Server) IDs, User IDs, Role IDs, and Channel IDs.
*   **Moderation Data:** If you receive a warning via the Bot, the reason for the warning and your User ID are stored.
*   **Server Configuration:** Settings such as mod-log channels, leveling/XP configuration, starboard thresholds, and AutoMod preferences are stored per-server.
*   **Message Content (Ephemeral):** The Bot reads message content for AutoMod features (e.g., spam and invite detection) and commands. *Message content is processed in real-time and is NEVER stored or logged persistently.*

## 2. How We Use Your Data
The data collected is strictly used to provide the Bot's advertised features:
*   Tracking user warnings for server moderators.
*   Storing leveling/XP progress and economy balances.
*   Managing support tickets.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
*   All persistent data (warnings, configurations) is stored locally in an SQLite database.
*   We take reasonable measures to secure the database environment to prevent unauthorized access.
*   We do not share, sell, or distribute your data to any third parties.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
*   **Warnings:** Can be deleted at any time by server administrators using the `/clearwarnings` command.
*   **Server Data:** If you remove the Bot from your server, you may request the deletion of your server's configuration data by contacting the developer.

## 5. Changes to this Policy
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this document.

## 6. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about this policy or your data, please contact the Bot developer.
