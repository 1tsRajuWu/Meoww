{# ========================================================================== _theme.html — the theme control, in one place. Two surfaces offer the same four palettes, and they were offering them from two different copies: the site frame's own markup, and — on the market page — an `{% include "theme_picker.html" %}`, a template the redesign had already archived. That is why `/market/` answered 500 rather than a page. A control written twice drifts; a control written once cannot, so both the frame and the market page import these two macros. Two macros rather than one because the button and the panel have to sit in different places: the button belongs in the header's action row, and the panel is deliberately *outside* that row, since a panel that is a flex item reserves its width while it is closed. Both surfaces therefore call `theme_button()` in their own header and `theme_menu()` once, after it. The four values are the contract with `static/theme.js` — `system`, `light`, `dark`, `contrast` — and they are written here once, which is the point. ========================================================================== #} {% macro theme_button() %} {% endmacro %} {% macro theme_menu() %}
