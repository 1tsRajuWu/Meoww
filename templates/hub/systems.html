Turning them on

A switch is per server, and it is real

Flipping one does not just hide a card: the bot republishes that server's command list, so the command disappears from Discord's own picker rather than answering with a refusal. That is why the console shows two readings side by side — what this server's switches say, and what Discord actually came back with.
