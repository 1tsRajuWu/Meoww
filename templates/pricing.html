FAQ

Common Questions

Can I try Pro features for free?

Yes, all new servers get a 7-day free trial of Pro features automatically.

What payment methods do you accept?

We're setting up our payment processors right now. (Coming soon!)

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, at any time from the link on your Lemon Squeezy receipt. The plan stays active on your servers until the end of the period you already paid for, and the licence key in /bot plan stops working after that.

Is my data safe?

Yes, we use encrypted backups and only store what is strictly necessary to run the systems you enable.

What happens if I downgrade?

Your premium features will be disabled, but your data is kept safe for 30 days in case you decide to upgrade again.
