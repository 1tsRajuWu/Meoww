Radio, and nothing else

One command puts a station on — by name, by language, or by the country you want to hear — and it stays until somebody stops it. Nothing to search, nothing to link, and when a station goes quiet the bot asks it again before it says a word.

{# ------------------------------------------------------------------ #} {# What it does #} {# ------------------------------------------------------------------ #}

Playback

Three complaints, answered

There is nothing to guess about

A station is its address, so there is no search, no match to get wrong and no link to expire. Every URL on this page was fetched and listened to before it was written down, and the country entries had to corroborate their own country first — a Ukrainian internet station filed under the Emirates stays out, because a directory's label is a claim like any other.

A station that goes quiet is asked again

A dropped connection reaches the player looking exactly like a programme that finished, so the old behaviour moved on in silence and nobody was told. A live stream has no end, so an ended one is now a cut: the same station is opened again within a couple of seconds, and only if it will not come back does the room hear about it — with how long it played and what the engine said. A station that keeps dropping is news; a station that blipped once is not.

ffmpeg does the work, at the source's own rate

A station sends MP3 or AAC; Discord wants Opus, so the bot decodes and re-encodes — above the channel's own much lower default rather than down to it, in one command line written in one place, with reconnect flags so a merely interrupted stream is retried instead of treated as over. Volume is applied live when the host has libopus, and re-renders the stream when it does not, which the card says out loud.

The seat is held by default: the bot stays in the channel until somebody runs /radio stop.

A station is somebody else's programming, so an ad break in the middle of a conversation cannot be filtered out of it — but which stations get played can be chosen. The stations whose own sites say they carry no advertising are marked ad-free here, and that is what the radio picks from unless a server says otherwise.

🎛️ What is said out loud

That station went quiet. Said only after it was asked again and would not come back, with how long it played — one station doing it is that station, all of them is the host.

That station would not open. A different card from the one above, because "it stopped" and "it never started" are fixed differently.

Nothing carried advertising there. Said when the country or language somebody asked for has no ad-free station in it yet, so the pick came from the wider table instead — a preference is not allowed to leave a room with silence.

Every radio failure is also one entry in the operator's own log — once per reason, not once per station.

The limits

What it will not do

It will not play a song by name

That used to be the headline feature, and it was given up on purpose rather than fixed: resolving a named song means asking a media platform, and a platform that has decided a datacenter address is a robot answers every request with a check instead of a stream. Cookies and proxies paper over it until the next change. A station needs none of that, so the honest thing to offer is a table rather than a search box that fails a third of the time.

It will not invent a country

A country appears in the picker when one of its stations was verified and the station backed the claim — its own name or state names the capital, or it is served from that country's own domain. Where nothing qualified, the country is absent and the picker says so, which is a smaller list than a directory would give and the only one that plays.

It says when it cannot play at all

A host needs an audio decoder and Discord's own encryption library before a single frame can be sent. If either is missing — or if the host simply cannot reach a station from where it runs — the command answers with what is wrong and how to fix it, instead of joining your channel and playing silence.
